# Social Preview Brief

## Purpose

Make the repository recognizable as a data-platform and reliability project without relying on statistics or a production screenshot.

## Canvas

- Size: 1280 × 640 pixels.
- Safe area: central 1100 × 500 pixels.
- Must remain legible at approximately 320 pixels wide.

## Visual direction

- Background: deep neutral blue or charcoal.
- Accent: one restrained cyan or green used only for validation and quality states.
- Left side: four simple stacked blocks labeled `INPUT`, `VALIDATE`, `WAREHOUSE`, `QUALITY + REPORT`.
- Right side: title `Commerce Data Automation` and subtitle `Synthetic reference architecture`.
- Use geometric lines, not platform or company logos.

## Required exclusions

No metrics, badges, cloud-console screenshots, browser chrome, employer or customer names, repository activity, personal photo, currency, source-system brands, identifiers, URLs, or claims such as “production-grade.”

## Alt text

“Layered diagram showing synthetic operational inputs moving through validation into a governed warehouse, quality controls, and reporting.”

## Production note

Generate only from this brief or the safe Mermaid source. Do not adapt a real diagram or screenshot. Strip metadata before publication.
